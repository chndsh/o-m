Organization & Management

Course Code: ME 708 | Quick-Reference Exam Study Notes

Chapter 1: Introduction 20 hours

System approach, Principles of organization, Management functions/skills/models, Classical & Modern theories, Ownership forms, Structures, Purchasing & Marketing.

Chapter 2: Personnel Management 8 hours

Personnel functions, Policy development, Manpower planning, Recruitment vs Scientific selection, Training methods, Job analysis/evaluation/merit rating, Wages & incentives.

Chapter 3: Motivation, Leadership & Entrepreneurship 10 hours

Maslow, Alderfer ERG, McClelland, Herzberg, Vroom, Blake & Mouton grid, Leadership styles/theories, Entrepreneurship development & small scale unit setup.

Chapter 4: Case Studies 2 hours

Case study objectives, 5 diagnostic phases, 6 research steps, 5Ws & 1H problem analysis framework, Case types & limitations.

Chapter 5: Management Information System 5 hours

Data vs Information, 10 qualities, MIS evolution, Pyramidal hierarchy, IS classifications (TPS, MIS, DSS, ESS, OAS, ES), Functional support, Centralized vs Decentralized.
